Technologies available for licensing

Inventions owned by Peruvian institutions whose applications we drafted and filed before INDECOPI. Title belongs to each institution; if your company wants to explore a licence, we can put you in touch.

Patent granted
Automatic cutter for precise vegetable cuts
Utility model · Exp. 000589-2024/DIN
U. N. Intercultural de la Selva Central Juan Santos Atahualpa

Cuts vegetables with a grid of interchangeable blades. A pneumatic cylinder pushes the product and a vibrating hopper aligns it before the cut, so the size stays consistent piece after piece.

Patent granted
Portable counting machine
Utility model · Exp. 000590-2024/DIN
U. N. Intercultural de la Selva Central Juan Santos Atahualpa

Counts objects inside boxes or bags without relying on any fixed structure. A laser and a photodiode register each piece as it passes and the total shows on screen; it runs on its own battery.

Patent granted
Impedance detection in meat to determine its quality
Utility model · Exp. 001280-2024/DIN
Universidad Nacional de Frontera

A dielectric glove with electrodes measures impedance in meat to assess its quality. It does not invade the sample, so the cut keeps its commercial value, and it logs readings wirelessly.

Pending
Powder analysis by spectrometer
Invention patent · Exp. 000593-2025/DIN
Universidad Nacional de Frontera

A rotating sample holder and a linearly moving sensor read the powder from several points across the plane. It removes the blind spots left by fixed-position spectrometers.

Pending
Multifunction device to determine food quality
Utility model · Exp. 001947-2024/DIN
Universidad Nacional de Frontera

Brings together in a single handheld unit an array of odour sensors, an RGB camera and a thermal camera, to assess meat and other foods without taking the sample to a laboratory.

Patent granted
Odour detector with handle
Utility model · Exp. 001807-2024/DIN
Universidad Nacional de Frontera

An array of semiconductor sensors housed in a casing with a handle. It is built for long shifts on the plant floor: the grip reduces hand fatigue during continuous sample analysis.

Each file can be looked up by its number in the records of INDECOPI's Directorate of Inventions and New Technologies.
