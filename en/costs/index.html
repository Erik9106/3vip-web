What does it cost to register a trademark or a patent in Peru?

Every intellectual property filing has two separate costs: the official fee paid to the State through INDECOPI, and the professional fees of whoever drafts and handles the file. Below are the current official fees, in full, so you know exactly which part of the cost is not negotiable.

Patents and designs

In these three procedures you do not pay the full fee when filing. A percentage is due at the start, and the balance only if the file qualifies for substantive examination.

Procedure Due at filing Total fee
Invention patent 55% due at filing S/ 396.00 S/ 720.00
Utility model 70% due at filing S/ 226.80 S/ 324.00
Industrial design 60% due at filing S/ 216.00 S/ 360.00

Utility model or invention patent: the choice is not always yours

Looking only at the fees, the utility model seems the obvious option: it costs S/ 226.80 against S/ 396.00 and is resolved in half the time. But the figure is not chosen on price. The law defines what each one can protect, and in most cases it is the nature of the invention that decides.

A utility model is any new shape, configuration or arrangement of elements of a device, tool, instrument, mechanism or other object, or of a part thereof, that allows improved or different operation, use or manufacture of the object incorporating it, or that provides it with some utility, advantage or technical effect it did not have before. Decision 486, article 81

In other words: the utility model protects objects with a shape. And article 82 expressly excludes processes from this figure, along with everything already excluded from invention patentability. That is why a new formula, a compound, a pharmaceutical kit, a process or a complex system can only be protected by an invention patent, meeting its requirements: novelty, inventive step and industrial application.

Filing as a utility model something the law does not admit under that figure does not save money: it ends in refusal. If the application has already been filed and the subject matter allows it, article 83 permits converting it into an invention patent application.

Trademarks and distinctive signs

Applies to product and service marks, collective and certification marks, trade names and slogans. The fee is charged per class of the Nice Classification in which protection is sought.

Procedure Standard fee MYPE fee
First class S/ 534.90 S/ 401.20
Each additional class S/ 533.30 S/ 399.90

Copyright and software

This is where most mistakes happen, for two reasons: some procedures share a form but carry different fees, and some registrations cost considerably less through the Online Services Platform (SEL) instead of the filing desk.

Procedure Filing desk SEL platform
Software or computer programs S/ 357.70 Not available
Databases, compilations and anthologies Uses the same form as software, but is a different procedure S/ 177.90 Not available
Literary works S/ 238.10 S/ 90.10
Artistic works and works of applied art Painting, photography, sculpture, drawing, engraving, craft, plans and similar S/ 238.10 S/ 90.10
Audiovisual works and productions S/ 238.10 Not available

Three things almost nobody tells you

Patenting costs less upfront

Filing an invention patent does not cost S/ 720.00 upfront, but S/ 396.00. The balance is due only if your application reaches substantive examination. Many inventors rule out patenting over a cost they do not actually face at the start.

The right channel saves 62%

Registering a literary or artistic work costs S/ 238.10 at the filing desk, but S/ 90.10 through the SEL platform. Same registration, same legal effect. Note: this difference does not apply to software or trademarks.

MYPEs pay 25% less on trademarks

If your company is registered in REMYPE, with an active RUC in good standing, the trademark fee drops from S/ 534.90 to S/ 401.20. It is worth confirming your registration before filing, not after.
